# AISHELL-3 word recordings — Apache License 2.0

Isolated word recordings from the AISHELL-3 corpus by Beijing Shell Shell
Technology Co., Ltd. (https://www.openslr.org/93/), for HSK 7-9 words that
have no other recording. License: `LICENSE` (Apache License 2.0).

Reference: Yao Shi, Hui Bu, Xin Xu, Shaoji Zhang, Ming Li,
*AISHELL-3: A Multi-speaker Mandarin TTS Corpus and the Baselines*.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.11567

Modifications: taken from the 24 kHz, 48 kbps MP3 conversion in
mandarin-sentence-audio; 70 Hz high-pass, gain to -21 LUFS integrated loudness
(true peak at most -1.5 dBTP), re-encoded as 128 kbps MP3. The speech is unchanged.
